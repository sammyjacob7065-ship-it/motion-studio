
# ANIMATION LINES IN THE SCRIPT (the script controls everything)
A scene may carry `ANIMATION: <plain words>` and the brief may list `PART <name>: <picture>` pieces. The ANIMATION line is law: build EXACTLY what it says, whatever it is. You are NOT limited to the ready-made moves below: any object, any motion (open, fall, roll, swing, pour, bounce, shake, pop, spin, float, burst, splash, steam, grow, fly in, assemble) is built by you from layers.
In an animated scene the supplied or generated PRODUCT picture (a cut-out with a transparent background, listed as "PRODUCT of an animated scene") is the hero. Do NOT put a full-screen picture behind it: the gradient background, glow, particles and the camera are the stage. Start the animation at the moment the scene's words start (use the word times) and let it finish before the scene ends; hold the final pose until the scene ends.

## Ready-made moves = layer type "magic" (a shortcut; use one only when it is exactly what the script asks for)
`{"type":"magic","start":..,"end":..,"props":{"kind":"...","asset":"product file","color":"#accent","speed":1}}`
- box_open: a carton drops in, its flaps open by themselves, light bursts, the product rises out, the box drops away, the product hovers. Takes about 3.6 s at speed 1 (use "speed" 1.5 for 2.4 s). props: boxColor.
- table_rise: a podium/table rises from below, the product drops on it with a squash bounce and dust ring (props.then:"roll" makes it roll in instead). About 2.4 s. props: tableColor.
- drop: the product falls from above onto the floor with a bounce and shadow. About 1.5 s. roll_in: the product rolls in from the left and stops upright. About 2.5 s.
- cap_pop: a bottle drops in, its cap shakes, pops off with fizz, bubbles and mist, flies, bounces and rolls away. props: capX (0-1 across the bottle, default .5), capY (0-1 down from the top, default .05), capW (cap width as a share of the bottle, default .2), capColor, capless:true when the picture has no cap, popAt (second of the pop, default 1.55). About 3.4 s.
- spin_float: the product spins in and floats with glow rays and twinkles. fly_in: same, and props.items (other picture files) fly in and orbit it.
- Effects without a picture: burst (light burst), fizz (bubbles), splash (liquid droplets: props.splashColor, power, count), steam (rising smoke/steam: props.smokeColor). Position with props.x (pixels) and props.fy (0-1 of the height).
- props.speed (0.4-3) squeezes or stretches a move to fit the scene. y (0-1) moves the whole stage up or down.

## Custom animation = layer type "group" (a rig). Use this for ANYTHING not listed above.
`{"id":"g1","type":"group","start":..,"end":..,"x":540,"y":900,"anim":{...},"props":{"children":[ {layer}, {layer}, ... ],"perspective":1200}}`
- Children are normal layers (image, shape, text, icon, line, particles, group) placed RELATIVE to the group centre: child x,y = 0,0 is the centre of the group. A child inherits every move of the group (position, scale, rotation, tilt, fade). Children keep their own start/end and their own anim tracks. Groups can contain groups.
- anim tracks (on ANY layer, absolute seconds): x, y, scale, scaleX, scaleY (squash and stretch), rotate (degrees, around the pivot), rotateX, rotateY (3D tilt and flip), skewX, opacity, blur, width, height. Key = [time, value, ease?].
- props.origin = [50,0] sets the PIVOT in percent of the layer (0,0 top-left, 50,50 centre default). A lid hinged at the back edge of a box = origin [50,100] rotating rotateX; a door = origin [0,50] with rotateY; a pendulum = origin [50,0] with rotate.
- props.wiggle = {"pos":6,"rot":3,"freq":4}: a living shake (vibrating, shivering, hand-held).
- Easings: outBounce and inBounce (a real bounce), outElastic, spring, outBack (overshoot), in (accelerates like gravity), out/outExpo (decelerates).
- Recipes for motion: FALL = y keys with ease "in" (gravity), then an impact squash: scaleY 1 to 0.8 to 1 with scaleX 1 to 1.15 to 1 (ease outElastic) in 0.25 s, then a small bounce. ROLL = x moves and rotate follows distance (rotate = distance / radius x 57.3). OPEN = rotate or rotateX/rotateY with outBack. POP = scale 0 to 1 with outBack. FLOAT = y keys going up and down slowly (inOutQuad). Shake the camera on impacts with 2-3 quick camera keys (scale 1.03 and back).
- Effects around the move: magic splash (droplets) at the landing point, magic steam above hot things, particles burst for sparks, a flash shape or transition flash at big hits, a soft shadow ellipse (shape circle, dark, low fillOpacity) under falling objects that grows as they land.
- Pieces: use the supplied/generated PART pictures when the script lists them; when a needed piece has no picture, DRAW it from shapes (rect, circle, pill, triangle, ring, gradients, glow) in the brand colours. Never invent a picture file name.
- Sound: put sound effects on the beats of the animation (pop on a pop, hit on an impact, whoosh on a fast move, sparkle on glow).
